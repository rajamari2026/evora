Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Veoa handles information when you use the Veoa website and Android application. Veoa provides photo organisation and selective photo sharing. Please read this policy before creating an account or uploading content.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, account identifier and authentication information needed to create and secure your account. If you sign in with Google, Google provides basic account information such as your email address and profile information permitted by your Google account settings.
  • Photos and files: images and other files you choose to upload, send or share, together with the information needed to organise them into dates, events or albums.
  • Sharing information: recipient email addresses, share requests, acceptance status, download permissions and related settings.
  • Technical information: information processed by the hosting, authentication and storage services to operate, protect and troubleshoot Veoa, such as request and security logs.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, display your gallery, organise content, deliver sharing requests, enforce your sharing choices, provide downloads where allowed, maintain the service and respond to support requests.

3. Photos, privacy and sharing

Your content is intended to remain private to your account unless you choose to share it. A recipient must accept a sharing request before shared content becomes available to them. You can stop sharing; the application is designed to remove the shared copies when sharing is stopped. Download permissions depend on the setting selected by the sender. A recipient may still capture content displayed on a screen.

Veoa offers an optional end-to-end encryption feature for supported private sharing. When enabled and correctly used, the content is encrypted on the sender's device and is intended to be readable only by the sender and recipient. Encryption is optional; do not assume every upload or share is end-to-end encrypted.

4. Service providers

Veoa uses Supabase for authentication, database and file-storage services, and Google when you choose Google sign-in. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies. The Veoa website is hosted through Cloudflare. Information may be processed in locations outside your country depending on these providers' infrastructure.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete photos and albums using the controls provided in Veoa. When you stop a share, the shared copies are designed to be deleted. Some account, security or operational records may remain for a limited period where needed for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution or service integrity, including in provider backups and logs. Deleting a photo from Veoa does not recall copies that a recipient previously downloaded or captured.

If you need help with account-related data or a deletion request, contact us using the email below and include the email address associated with your Veoa account. We may need to verify that you control the account before acting.

6. Security

We use access controls and the security features provided by our infrastructure providers. No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your sign-in credentials and any optional encryption passcode private. If you lose an encryption passcode, encrypted content may not be recoverable.

7. Children

Veoa is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly invite children under 13 to create accounts. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Veoa changes. We will update the date at the top when changes are made. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use.
