Why people use Veoa

Photos grouped into events

Photos are organised by day. Name a day, or pick photos from different days, to build an event album.

Accept before anyone sees

When you share, the other person gets a request. They see nothing until they accept, and you can stop sharing at any time.

End-to-end encrypted option

Turn on private sharing and photos are locked on your device. Only you and the person you share with can open them.

View, download and send in a browser

Sign in on any computer to view shared photos, download one photo or a whole ZIP when the sender allows it, and send photos too.

You choose: view only or download

Every share has its own setting. View-only shares hide the save button and use a lower-quality copy.

Built for Android

The Veoa Android app reads your phone gallery, supports pinch zoom, swipe select and sends photos in batches.

How Veoa photo sharing works

  1. Pick photos. Select a few photos or a whole event in the Veoa app, or choose files in your browser.
  2. Send to a person. Enter their email once. Next time just pick their name.
  3. They accept. They sign in to Veoa on the web or the app, accept the request and see the photos.
  4. View or download. If you allowed downloads they can save single photos or the whole album as a ZIP.

Questions about Veoa

Is Veoa free?

Yes, Veoa is free to use.

Are my photos private?

Photos are shared only with the people you pick. They must accept first and you can stop sharing at any time, which deletes the shared copies. With end-to-end encryption on, only you and the receiver can open the photos, not even the server.

Can people download my photos?

Only if you allow it for that share. Anyone who can see a photo on their screen could still capture it, so share view-only photos only with people you trust.

Does Veoa work on iPhone?

The Veoa mobile app is for Android. The website works in any modern browser on a computer, iPhone or Android phone.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Create a free account with your email so the right person receives the photos you share.
